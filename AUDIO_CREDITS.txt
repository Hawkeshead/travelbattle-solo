  Four takes of an officer calling fire and his squad volleying, one picked at

  128k for size (1.5 MB each to 64 KB, no audible loss at four seconds).
  SOURCE AND LICENCE STILL TO BE RECORDED HERE.

UNIT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT, BRITISH. audio/effects/ack-british-1.m4a through -4.m4a.
  Plays on selecting a British INFANTRY or GUARD unit, replacing the
  piece-placed click. -1 is Freesound 402020 "Yes Sir (British male)" by
  theuncertainman — CHECK AND RECORD THE LICENCE AT
  https://freesound.org/s/402020/ , since Freesound is per-sound and is either
  CC0 or CC-BY depending on the upload. If it is CC-BY it needs an attribution
  line in the game, not just in this file. -2 to -4 appear to be pitch and pace
  variants of the same recording and inherit whatever -1 carries.
  Re-encoded from WAV to AAC 128k (1.5 MB each to about 22 KB).
