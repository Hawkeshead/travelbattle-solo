NO PEAK. The spread across the whole range 2.0 to 6.0 is 48% to 53.8%, and
the 95% band on 26 matches is about +/-19 points, so every value here is the
same value within the noise. The current setting of 5.0 is not wrong and no
change is recommended.

WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT THE SWEEP SET. 26 matches per value cannot separate
differences of this size; it would take roughly 400 per value to resolve five
points. Either sweep terms where a wrong value is catastrophic rather than
marginal, or accept that the answer to 'what should this weight be' is often
'it does not matter much', which is itself worth knowing after four builds of
hand-tuning this one.

NOT TO BE APPLIED TO MAIN ON THE STRENGTH OF ONE SWEEP. Terms interact; the
set must be re-swept with the others at their new values first.
