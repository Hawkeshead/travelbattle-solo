## Images — PROVENANCE NOT YET ESTABLISHED

`js/render-units.js:26` records that the 24 regiment portraits in
`assets/portraits/` were *"imported from the TravelBattle Hub Archive"*. No
original source, artist, or licence was recorded for any of them. The same
applies to the 6 commander portraits in `assets/brigadiers/` and the 2 cannon
icons in `assets/icons/`.

**Until the table below is filled in, treat these images as all-rights-reserved
and do not reuse them.**

The risk is specific and worth stating plainly. If these are photographs of
period paintings, they are almost certainly fine: the artists died well over 70
years ago, and a faithful flat reproduction of a public-domain work attracts no
new copyright in the UK, EU or US. But Napoleonic *uniform plates* — the Osprey
Men-at-Arms style of illustration — are modern works, firmly in copyright, and
are exactly the kind of image a wargame reaches for. The two cannot be told
apart from the files alone.

This repository is public, and once someone forks it the history is permanent.
Any image that cannot be cleared should be replaced or removed sooner rather
than later.

## The board game itself

Game rules, mechanics and systems are not protected by copyright, so a rules
adaptation is on solid ground. What *is* protected is the game's **name**, its
trade dress, and any verbatim rulebook **text**. The safe posture, already
adopted in the README, is: describe the project as an unofficial fan adaptation,
don't reproduce rulebook prose word-for-word, and don't monetise it.
