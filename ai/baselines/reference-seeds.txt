READ THIS BEFORE TREATING A SEED AS A FIXTURE

A HUMAN MATCH CANNOT BE REPLAYED by the simulator: the human's moves are not in
the seed. Simulator batches DO reproduce exactly (a run.mjs batch repeated on
21 Sep gave identical stalls and turn counts). Earlier notes here claimed the
seed did not fix the map; matches are now seeded throughout.

term-report.mjs must run ONE seed per process. Several in one process
contaminate each other (a match "finished" on turn 1 in one batched run).

This is why the Cerberus term profile beside this file is a multi-match average
rather than a single match.
