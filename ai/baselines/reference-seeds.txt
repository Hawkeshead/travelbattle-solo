READ THIS BEFORE TREATING A SEED AS A FIXTURE

The seed fixes the DICE, not the MAP. Terrain comes from a separate source, so
re-running a seed plays a different battle on different ground. A seed
reproduces a run within a batch; it does not reproduce a past match. Seed 5
timed out at turn 2380 in one batch and finished at turn 82 in the next.

This is why the Cerberus term profile beside this file is a multi-match average
rather than a single-seed capture. A one-seed term table would not reproduce,
and a baseline that cannot be re-derived is not a baseline.
