ALEXANDER — BASELINE NOTES
AI baseline as of 22 September 2026. Git tag `alexander`.
Saladin plus the army plan, SHELTER, the finishing rule and two stall breaks.

Load as a fixed opponent:   node tools/sim/run.mjs 100 --vs alexander

------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS NEW SINCE SALADIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE ARMY PLAN (on). The army decides which two enemy Brigades it intends to
break and what each of its own Brigades is for, rather than coordinating by
coincidence.
  - difficulty = their toughness (numbers, with Guard, Heavy Cavalry and guns
    counting extra) less the best superiority any ONE of our Brigades has over
    them, in numbers AND in type. Type comes from estimateFightValue, the same
    estimator the fight phase uses.
  - the two easiest become primary and secondary; the third is deliberately not
    attacked. Strongest Brigade STRIKES the primary, next SUPPORTS, the third
    FIXES the non-target. The strongest is never sent to FIX.
  - revised only when a Brigade breaks or a Brigadier is lost.
  - Brigades on PRESERVE, SHELTER, WITHDRAW, REUNITE or FETCH are exempt and
    keep their own mission.
  Measured plan-on against the same build plan-off, sides swapped, 50 matches:
  65.2% of 46 decided (95% range 51 to 79).

SHELTER (on). A Brigade down to 30% or fewer of the units it started with falls
back beside the strongest healthy Brigade, prefers cover, keeps firing and takes
favourable fights. Below two fighters PRESERVE takes over as before.
  Measured against saladin: 45% over 46 matches, so not a win-rate gain. It is
  the behaviour asked for at roughly no cost: the losing side takes an enemy
  Brigade down with it in 37% of its losses, against 12% before.

THE FINISHING RULE (on). Cavalry within 6 and infantry within 4 converge on an
enemy Brigade reduced to its last fighting unit; guns with line of sight at 5+
close to range 3-4; the kill is worth +6, or +8 when it wins the match. Expires
after four turns without progress, then rests six.
  Measured against saladin: 50% over 26 matches. Recruited units close 46% of
  the time against 24% for units it does not recruit.

TWO STALL BREAKS (on).
  - An army cannot preserve its way to victory: when every surviving Brigade is
    at PRESERVE, the strongest stops hiding.
  - When no Brigade has had a mission that initiates for twenty turns, the
    strongest attacks.
  Stalls fell from 8 of 50 to 4 of 50 with the plan acting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALEXANDER AGAINST SALADIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------

  49 decided matches, sides swapped:  26 wins, 53%

Read that honestly. The army plan is clearly better than no army plan within the
same build (65%), but Alexander overall is only slightly ahead of Saladin,
because SHELTER costs a little and the finishing rule is level. The plan is the
gain; the other two are behaviour changes that were asked for and paid for.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWN AND UNFIXED
------------------------------------------------------------------------

UNITS WILL NOT CLOSE. The largest remaining fault, and the cause of every
stall left. A Brigade ordered to attack can sit six squares from the enemy for
250 turns (seed 4). Measured during the finishing work: of units able to move
and within four tiles of a cornered enemy, only about 30% closed, and in four
cases out of five a closer legal square was available and was not taken. The
closer square is usually not even in the unit's top four options, so this is not
a weight that wants nudging.

Also open: brigade-level plans (where each Brigade takes and holds ground), the
brigade deployment UI, and the mute and volume controls.
