SALADIN — TERM PROFILE AND MISSION NOTES
AI baseline as of 21 September 2026. Git tag `saladin`, commit 18f49ae.
Cerberus plus the Brigadier recovery fix and the FETCH and REUNITE missions.

Load as a fixed opponent:   node tools/sim/run.mjs 100 --vs saladin

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1. THE TWO MISSIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Both are Brigadier recovery errands. They exist because reconnecting a
scattered Brigade can take several turns, and a decision that takes several
turns has to be committed to rather than re-scored every turn.

WHEN EITHER IS ASSIGNED
  More than 40% of a Brigade's remaining units are off the chain (cannot move
  because they are out of reach of the Brigadier). At or below 40% he leaves
  them and the Brigade keeps doing its job.

  Only ONE Brigade per side can be on an errand at once. The other two keep
  their army-level missions.

  WITHDRAW and PRESERVE Brigades are never given an errand.

WHICH OF THE TWO
  Count the enemy units within 2 tiles of the connected units he would leave
  behind (flat headcount, cavalry not weighted, since infantry form square).
    that count >= half the connected units   ->  REUNITE
    otherwise                                ->  FETCH

  REUNITE  the whole Brigade falls back onto the cut-off group.
  FETCH    the connected units hold their ground; the Brigadier goes alone.

WHAT HE GOES TO
  A group, not a single unit: cut-off units within 2 tiles of each other are
  one group, because arriving beside two frees two.
  Groups are ranked on value and proximity with EQUAL weight (a line unit back
  this turn can beat heavy cavalry back next turn). Longest wait breaks a tie;
  a coin flip from the seeded dice breaks what is left, so matches replay.
  Recovery value is its own table, not AI_UNIT_VALUE:
    HEAVY_CAV 5, GUARD 4.5, LIGHT_CAV 4, INFANTRY 3, ARTILLERY 0.5
  Artillery is deliberately last: a gun left off the chain on a vantage point
  is normal play, not an accident.

HOW LONG
  Up to 4 turns. Ended early only by success (all targets reconnected) or
  death (targets destroyed, or the Brigadier lost). A casualty changing the
  40% ratio cannot cancel one in flight, because running errands are settled
  before the ratio is recomputed.
  One failsafe: a FETCH upgrades to a REUNITE if the units left behind come
  under pressure. Never the reverse.
  An errand outranks the army plan while it runs.

missionPull TARGET
  The rally point is the centroid of the cut-off group still alive, recomputed
  each turn (it only moves when one of them dies).
    Brigadier, both missions:   -distance * APPROACH_PULL
                                + max(0, 4 - distance) * 0.20
                                (the close-range term stops him halting two
                                squares short, which frees nobody)
    Other units, REUNITE:       -distance * APPROACH_PULL * 0.6
    Other units, FETCH:          0  (they hold where they stand)

MOVE ORDER
  Unchanged from before: when a Brigade is split, connected units move first,
  then the Brigadier, then the cut-off group. Because he moves after his men
  and is pulled toward the strays, he steps back behind the line on the
  diagonal without any hard-coded idea of "backwards".

In the reference match: FETCH assigned 38 times, REUNITE 12.

READ WITH CARE
  87 sampled moves is thin. Matches no longer stall, so they are short, and the
  report samples on a timer that misses turns in fast matches (see the caveat
  in term-report.mjs). Against Cerberus (687 moves, two of three matches
  stalled) the comparison that holds up is the SHAPE, not the percentages:
    - stall terms fell from the top: brigadierTrail 22.5% -> 11.4%,
      freeStrandedUnit 18.7% -> 13.7%
    - threat rose from 1.7% to 20.6%: the armies are now close enough to each
      other for danger to decide moves, which in Cerberus it almost never did
    - guns firing from range 5-6: 58.9% -> 47.3%

  Nearly half of all artillery fire is still from range 5 or 6, which bears
  directly on the finishing-rule work that follows this tag.
